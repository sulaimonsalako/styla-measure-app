Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Styla ("we," "us") helps you find clothing that fits your body. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to www.styla.ca, the Styla browser extension and bookmarklet, and the Styla fit widget where it appears on a retailer's online store.

Who this policy is for

Styla is used by two groups, and the relationship is different for each.

  • Shoppers. When you use styla.ca or save a Styla account, we decide how your information is used, and this policy governs it in full.
  • Retailers, and shoppers using the widget on a retailer's store. When a retailer installs Styla on their store, they decide to offer it and we process shopper information on their behalf. In data-protection terms the retailer is the controller and Styla is the processor. The retailer's own privacy policy also applies, and our agreement with them governs what we may do with that information.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and password (stored securely, hashed).
  • Fit profile — the answers you give in our questionnaire and any measurements or fit preferences you save, which we use to recommend sizes.
  • Measurements entered without an account — if you use the fit widget on a retailer's store as a guest, the measurements needed to size you are stored in your own browser on that retailer's website, so we can answer without asking you again. See "Guest measurements" below.
  • Sharing information — if you share your size with someone (for gifting or a stylist), the email address of the person you share with and the status of that share.
  • Payment information — when you make a purchase, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card details.
  • Retailer information — when a retailer installs the Styla app, we read their product catalogue, inventory, shipping settings and published theme in order to recommend sizes on their product pages, together with the size charts they upload and their app settings. We do not request access to a retailer's customer records or orders.
  • Usage data — basic technical information such as device, browser, and pages visited, used to operate and improve the service.

Photographs

Styla does not ask for, take, or process photographs of you. Sizes are worked out from the answers and measurements you provide yourself. If we ever add a camera-based option, it will be optional, we will say who processes the images before you use it, and we will update this policy first.

Guest measurements

You can use the fit widget without creating an account. When you do, the measurements you provide are kept in your browser's local storage on that retailer's website, and are sent to Styla only to calculate a size. They stay on that site — they do not follow you to other stores, which is what a Styla account is for. They remain until you clear them, clear your browser's site data for that store, or the browser discards them. If you share a device, anyone else using that browser on that store may see the size it produces.

How we use your information

  • To build your fit profile and recommend brands and sizes that fit you.
  • To enable size sharing with people you choose.
  • To process payments and provide access to paid features.
  • To send you service emails (confirmations, receipts, and share invites you initiate).
  • To secure, maintain, and improve the service.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to serve advertising. We share it only with the service providers that make Styla work, and only as much as each one needs:

  • Supabase — database and authentication.
  • Google (Gemini) — powers the fit assistant and the product search behind it. Your question and the relevant product and size-chart details are sent to answer it.
  • Vercel — hosting.
  • Stripe — payments.
  • Shopify — where a retailer runs their store on Shopify, for the app to function on their product pages.
  • Our email provider, for service messages and share invitations you initiate.

Your fit profile is shared with another person only when you explicitly choose to share it. We do not share one retailer's information with another.

Automated size recommendations

Sizes are calculated automatically by comparing your measurements against a brand's published size chart. No decision with a legal or similarly significant effect is made about you. Every recommendation shows the reasoning behind it, and you can always disregard it.

Cookies

We use essential cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in and to operate the service. The fit widget on a retailer's store uses your browser's local storage, not advertising cookies, and only to remember the size information you gave it. You can control cookies and clear site data through your browser settings.

Data retention

  • Your account and fit profile — kept while your account is active. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Guest measurements — held in your own browser until you clear them; see "Guest measurements" above.
  • Retailer information — when a retailer uninstalls the Styla app, Shopify notifies us 48 hours later and we erase that store's catalogue, size charts, settings and session data from our systems. Retailers who reinstall after that point start fresh.
  • Privacy requests — we keep a record that a request was received and actioned, so we can demonstrate we handled it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Canada's PIPEDA and, where applicable, the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

Children

Styla is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with an updated date.
