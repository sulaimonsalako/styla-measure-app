Styla ("we," "us") helps you find clothing that fits your body. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to www.styla.ca and related services.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and password (stored securely, hashed).
  • Fit profile — the answers you give in our questionnaire and any measurements or fit preferences you save, which we use to recommend sizes.
  • Sharing information — if you share your size with someone (for gifting or a stylist), the email address of the person you share with and the status of that share.
  • Payment information — when you make a purchase, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card details.
  • Usage data — basic technical information such as device, browser, and pages visited, used to operate and improve the service.

How we use your information

  • To build your fit profile and recommend brands and sizes that fit you.
  • To enable size sharing with people you choose.
  • To process payments and provide access to paid features.
  • To send you service emails (confirmations, receipts, and share invites you initiate).
  • To secure, maintain, and improve the service.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Styla — such as Supabase (database and authentication), Stripe (payments), and our email provider — and only as needed to deliver the service. Your fit profile is shared with another person only when you explicitly choose to share it.

Cookies

We use essential cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in and to operate the service. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Canada's PIPEDA and, where applicable, the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

Children

Styla is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with an updated date.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email contact@styla.ca.
