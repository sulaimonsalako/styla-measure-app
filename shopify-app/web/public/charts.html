Store:

What Styla does for your store

Two separate things — use either or both.

Get recommended on Styla Free

Styla shoppers get matched to clothes that fit them across brands. With this on, your products can be recommended to them — new customers, no cost. Turn it off and your catalog is never shown outside your own store.

Size widget on your product pages Paid

Shows every shopper their correct size on your product pages, with the AI fit chat. Turn this off to keep only the free sharing above.

Product sync

Lets Styla's AI recommend other items that fit each shopper, and lets you assign charts to individual products.

New and edited products sync automatically after the first run.
