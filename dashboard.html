📏 How to measure yourself (2-minute guide)

Use a soft tape measure over light clothing or underwear. Keep the tape level and snug — not tight. Ask someone to help if you can.

Chest / Bust
Around the fullest part of your chest/bust, tape level under the arms and across the shoulder blades.
Waist
Around your natural waistline — the narrowest point, usually just above the belly button. Don't suck in.
Hips
Around the fullest part of your hips and seat, feet together, tape level all the way around.
Inseam
From the top of your inner thigh straight down to the ankle bone — or measure a pair of trousers that fit well.
Neck
Around the base of your neck where a collar sits, with one finger inside the tape for comfort.
Belly
Around the fullest part of your midsection, over the belly button. Relax — don't hold your breath.
Shoulder width
Straight across the back, from the corner of one shoulder to the other — where jacket seams would sit.
Sleeve length
From the shoulder corner, down a slightly bent arm, to the wrist bone.
Thigh
Around the fullest part of one thigh, just below the seat, standing naturally.
